About

Eastbound Reports provides weekly economic and financial analysis from international media sources across Russia, China, Japan, Korea, and North Korea.

Each week, we automatically fetch articles from 12 major news sources and use AI to identify key economic and financial developments, distilling them into concise 1-2 sentence summaries that capture trends across all monitored regions.

Our summaries focus specifically on economic implications: policy changes, trade relationships, sanctions, energy markets, currency movements, corporate developments, and infrastructure projects.

Summaries are posted weekly to Twitter/X and LinkedIn.

Coverage

  • Russian Media (4 sources): TASS, RT, Sputnik, RIAN
  • Chinese Media (3 sources): Xinhua, People's Daily, CGTN
  • Japanese Media (2 sources): NHK, Japan Times
  • Korean Media (1 source): Yonhap
  • North Korean Analysis (2 sources): 38 North, Daily NK

How It Works

  • Automated RSS feed monitoring of 12 international news sources
  • Full article content extraction (not just headlines)
  • AI-powered financial analysis using Claude Code
  • Geographic diversity enforcement across all regions
  • Weekly posting to social media and GitHub Pages (every Monday)
  • 100% open source

Open Source

All code is available on GitHub. Feel free to fork, contribute, or use for your own projects.
